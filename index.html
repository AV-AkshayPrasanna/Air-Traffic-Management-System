Project

About the Project

Flight Tracking

Records every aircraft in the airspace with its position, altitude and heading, and checks callsigns through a hash set.

Landing Priority Queue

Orders arriving flights in a min-heap so an emergency always lands before a routine flight.

Conflict Detection

Compares every pair of aircraft and flags those closer than the separation minimum at the same altitude.

Route Planning

Finds the shortest route between airports on a weighted graph using Dijkstra's algorithm.

Radar

Airspace radar

Aircraft cross the sector in real time. Every frame the system compares all pairs and circles any two that are inside the separation minimum at the same altitude.

Scale: 1 NM is 5 px. Range rings every 20 NM. Click an aircraft, or pick it from the list, to see its details and hand it to the approach queue.

        Routes

        Route planner

        Nine airports form a weighted graph. Dijkstra's algorithm uses the same min-heap to find the shortest route. Click an airport on the map to close it and the route is worked out again.

        Distances are approximate great-circle kilometres. Dashed red airports are closed.

        Globe

        Network globe

        The same nine-airport graph on a 3D globe. Drag to rotate. Click one airport for the origin and another for the destination, and Dijkstra's shortest route is drawn in gold. The globe and the route planner above stay in sync.

        Drag or use the arrow keys to rotate, pinch or the slider to zoom. Gold arcs are the current shortest route; faint arcs are other legs.
